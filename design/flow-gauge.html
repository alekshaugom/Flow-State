Flow-range gauge

The signature flow-status graphic from the Flow State app, isolated for you to alter. Pure SVG — edit the CONFIG block in the script, or grab the SVG markup under each gauge.

One continuous system. The ring is a faint gray base; the colored outline rises from the bottom to currentFlow ÷ top-of-good, and once it's full the shape keeps morphing:
  • Too low → Good — open horseshoe; the colored outline rises with flow (yellow, then green).
  • High — outline is full; the U's top endpoints begin to bend outward, more as flow climbs (red).
  • Flood — top endpoints fully bent out; the gauge pulses (deep red).
The CFS value sits in the empty center, in the band color. The rounded bottom of every state is the same semicircle (radius = the ring), and the flare is built on that same radius.

To alter: edit the CONFIG object at the top of the <script> — colors, stroke, ring size, horseshoe opening (gap), U height, the flare spread, type, and the pulse. The “SVG markup” toggles give copy-ready vectors for Figma/Illustrator.
