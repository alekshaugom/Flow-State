Components

Map

Two distinct map engines. The state overview (Leaflet, grayscaled OpenStreetMap tiles) draws every river section as a status-colored line. The corridor detail (MapLibre, light "positron" basemap) draws one navy section line with put-in / take-out / dam / gauge markers and a signature 270° "gauge donut" for live CFS. Specimens below are static reproductions of those visual elements.

Navigation & hero

The sticky blurred header, and the frosted-glass search hero — the single most premium element in the product. Its depth/blur language doesn't yet carry into the rest of the app.
